My Obsidian had ten plugins doing ten small jobs, each with its own settings and its own look. So I rebuilt the ones I use every day as one plugin. It's called **Snailkit**, and it's free.

I vibe coded it over a few weeks with two AI developers: Claude Code plans and builds, Codex (OpenAI) takes some of the work and reviews what Claude writes. I decide, test, and say no a lot.

![Snailkit Tasks in Obsidian](/assets/2026/snailkit/tasks.webp){: .image80 }

## Why

I like writing my tasks right where I think: in a meeting note, a project note, my daily note. A checkbox, a tag, done. For years a [Dataview query](/obsidian/Obsdian-dataview-tasks) gathered them for me. I don't use it anymore: Snailkit does the same job, better, and the way I work. Less friction. I can think, drop my ideas, and get them off my mind.

## The big three

- **Brainstorm.** Write everything on your mind in one note. Later, sort it line by line: a task, a question to decide, or just an idea.
- **Tasks.** Write `- [ ] Call the bank #home` in any note. It shows up in one list, grouped by tag, with Today and Upcoming.
- **Home.** Today's note, what's due, your pins, and a map of your notes.

![The map of Home](/assets/2026/snailkit/home-map.webp){: .image80 }

## And a few small tools

Each one starts off. You turn on what you want.

- **Note menu**: a small row of icons next to your note: its outline, your pins, its open tasks and the calendar.
- **Slash menu**: type `/` to insert a block, a snippet or a command without leaving your sentence.
- **Slides to PowerPoint**: turns the frames of an Excalidraw drawing into a .pptx.
- **PDF export**: your note as a PDF, with your theme, images and clickable links (desktop).
- **Tables**, **Tag colors**, **Move text**, **Column select**: small things that make writing smoother.

I'll show some of them in their own short posts.

## On a phone too

I use it a lot on my iPhone, so the phone gets a lighter layout. One tap to add a task, a long press for the rest.

![Snailkit Tasks on a phone](/assets/2026/snailkit/phone-tasks.webp){: .image50 }

## Try it

{: .note }
> In Obsidian: **Settings → Community plugins → Browse**, search **Snailkit**, install, enable.
> Then write a task with a `#tag` in any note. It's already waiting in the list.

Everything stays in your notes, in plain Markdown. Nothing is sent anywhere.

The code is on [GitHub](https://github.com/arnaudgastelblum/snailkit). If something feels off, [tell me](https://github.com/arnaudgastelblum/snailkit/issues).
