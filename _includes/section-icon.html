{%- comment -%} The icon of a blog section, drawn inline so it takes the colour of its parent (currentColor). Usage: {% include section-icon.html key="power-bi" %} with key power-bi, sql-server, ai, obsidian or other (plus tools, the same wrench as other, and snail, the brand's shell). Set its size and colour in CSS on .section-icon. One family: 24 px grid, 1.75 px round strokes, a second tone as a low-opacity fill of the same colour. power-bi: a star schema, the fact table in the middle and four dimensions around it. sql-server: a database, the top disc lifted. ai: a terminal prompt with a spark biting its corner. obsidian: a note with a folded corner, a task ticked. other: a wrench, as its articles are shown as "Tools". snail: the shell, a spiral, kept for later. {%- endcomment -%}
